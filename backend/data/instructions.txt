1 1 Peel and chop the onion.
1 2 Boil water with a little salt and cook the pasta for 8-10 minutes.
1 3 Fry the onion in the oil for 3-4 minutes.
1 4 Add the minced beef and fry it until it is brown, about 6-8 minutes.
1 5 Add the chopped tomatoes, the rest of the salt and the pepper. Let it simmer for 10 minutes.
1 6 Drain the pasta and serve it with the sauce.

2 1 Cook the rice as it says on the package.
2 2 Cut the chicken into small pieces and the broccoli into small florets.
2 3 Fry the chicken in the oil for about 6 minutes, until it is cooked through.
2 4 Add the broccoli and fry for 4 more minutes.
2 5 Add the soy sauce and stir. Serve with the rice.

3 1 Cook the rice as it says on the package.
3 2 Peel and chop the onion and fry it in the oil for 3 minutes.
3 3 Add the minced beef and fry it until it is brown.
3 4 Add the salt, pepper, paprika, cumin and chilli powder and stir for 1 minute.
3 5 Add the chopped tomatoes and the kidney beans. Let it simmer for 15 minutes.
3 6 Serve with the rice.

4 1 Cook the rice as it says on the package.
4 2 Peel the onion and chop it together with the red pepper.
4 3 Fry the onion and the pepper in the oil for 5 minutes.
4 4 Add the salt, pepper, paprika, cumin and chilli powder and stir for 1 minute.
4 5 Add the chopped tomatoes, the kidney beans and the sweetcorn. Let it simmer for 15 minutes.
4 6 Serve with the rice.

5 1 Cut the chicken into strips and fry them in the oil for 6-8 minutes. Add salt and pepper.
5 2 Tear the lettuce into pieces and put it in a bowl.
5 3 Add the chicken, the croutons and the dressing.
5 4 Grate the parmesan on top.
